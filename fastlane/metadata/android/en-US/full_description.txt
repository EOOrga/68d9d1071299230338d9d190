OTHMAN GOLD CAR helps car rental teams keep daily operations organized without accounts or cloud sync. The app combines reservation management, rental contract tracking, vehicle check-in/check-out inspection checklists, and customer records in one focused mobile workspace.

Create local records, assign a responsible agent, track pickup and return windows, update progress, and review status from dashboard and reporting views. Records stay on the device so teams can manage the rental fleet with a simple, repeatable workflow.
